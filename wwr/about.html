River Moroto

We are passionate about helping people experience the excitement of the river while enjoying the beauty of Lira and the surrounding landscapes. Our guides create safe, fun, and unforgettable adventures for beginners and seasoned adventurers alike.

History

River Moroto began with a simple idea: share the thrill of whitewater rafting with friends, families, and travelers from around the world. Since our early days, we have grown into a trusted local company known for our friendly guides, memorable trips, and commitment to safety.

Our company was founded to give visitors a chance to explore Uganda's incredible rivers, vibrant communities, and unforgettable scenery. We built our reputation on hospitality, professionalism, and responsible adventure tourism.

Today, River Moroto continues to welcome adventurers who want to challenge themselves, enjoy nature, and create lasting memories on the water.
