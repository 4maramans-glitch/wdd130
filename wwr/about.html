River Moroto

We are passionate about helping people experience the excitement of the river while enjoying the beauty of Uganda. Our guides create safe, fun, and unforgettable adventures for beginners and seasoned adventurers alike.

History

River Moroto began with a simple idea: share the thrill of whitewater rafting with friends, families, and travelers from around the world. Since our early days, we have grown into a trusted local company known for our friendly guides, memorable trips, and commitment to safety on the River Moroto.
